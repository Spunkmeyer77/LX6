LX-6 - six-voice digital drum synthesizer by Ticco Ross

This installs:
  - the Audio Unit  (/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components)
  - the VST3 plugin  (/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3)
  - the standalone app  (/Applications)

LX-6's sound engine is based on the open-source Erica Synths LXR firmware.
LX-6 is an independent product and is not made or endorsed by Erica Synths.
